# Limitations

- Only the 12-month horizon is primary; 24-month coverage is low and 36-month coverage is absent.
- Forecast skill is modest rather than production-grade.
- No historical optimal-share labels exist for the general study population.
- No historical offer-price dataset is available; offers are scenario inputs.
- R0 is not supported by its held-out decision evaluation.
- The dimensional diagnosis and all R1-R5 comparisons are post-hoc.
- R4 was developed and evaluated on the already-open test set and requires fresh-data validation.
- R4's evidence is mixed within its own genuinely partial-sale scenarios.
- Risk preferences were not calibrated to a real club.
- The framework does not estimate causal effects of selling different percentages.
- Legal and contractual treatment of economic rights is abstracted rather than contract-specific.
- Raw and row-level data are excluded because redistribution rights are not established.
